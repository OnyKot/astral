# About Astral

Astral is a communication platform for communities, creators, friends, and teams who want chat, voice, media, and workspaces to feel fast and focused.

We are building Astral around a simple idea: people should be able to talk, create, organize, and share without fighting the interface. The product is designed to feel calm, responsive, and expressive whether you open it on the web, Windows, Android, or a mobile browser.

## What We Are Building

- **Messenger-first communication:** direct messages, communities, channels, voice, media, and presence in one clear flow.
- **A calmer workspace:** fewer noisy layers, clearer navigation, and tools that keep attention on people and conversations.
- **Native and web access:** Windows and Android builds are available now, while the web app keeps Astral accessible across other platforms.
- **A platform for growth:** communities, creator tools, moderation, discovery, and integrations are developed as one connected ecosystem.

## Product Direction

Astral is moving toward a modern messenger experience with strong performance, reliable infrastructure, and a visual identity that feels spacious, dark, and focused. We care about speed, readable interfaces, predictable actions, and a design language that can scale across the app and the marketing site.
